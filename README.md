# Simple Interest Calculator

## Project Overview

The Simple Interest Calculator is a beginner-friendly project that calculates the simple interest earned or paid on a principal amount over a specified period at a given annual rate of interest.

## Formula

**Simple Interest = (Principal × Rate × Time) / 100**

Where:
- **Principal** is the initial amount of money.
- **Rate** is the annual rate of interest, expressed as a percentage.
- **Time** is the duration in years.

## Example

If the principal is 1,000, the annual interest rate is 5%, and the time period is 2 years:

Simple Interest = (1,000 × 5 × 2) / 100 = 100

The simple interest is **100**, and the total amount is **1,100**.

## How to Use

1. Enter the principal amount.
2. Enter the annual rate of interest as a percentage.
3. Enter the time period in years.
4. Calculate the simple interest using the formula above.

This project is intended for learning basic calculations and introductory scripting concepts.
